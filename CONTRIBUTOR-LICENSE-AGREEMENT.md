## Signing

Fill this in and include it in your first pull request as
`contributors/<your-github-username>.md`. One signature covers every
Contribution you make afterwards, so you do this once.

```
Full name:
GitHub username:
Email:
Date:

I have read the backupd Individual Contributor License Agreement and I
agree to it for this and all my future Contributions to this project.

Signed:
```

Typing your name into the `Signed:` line, in a commit authored by you, is the
signature. There is nothing to print, scan or post.
