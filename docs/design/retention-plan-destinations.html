A retention plan picks its destinations

A plan already says how long to keep a copy. This adds where the copy lives, chosen from the same three kinds of place in the global plan and in a backup set's own: this machine, a destination the deployment already declares, or one configured through the wizard for this plan alone. The plan sets a default; a tier may override it, because the schema this writes to is per tier.

PREVIEW
Changing where a tier sends copies does not move the copies it already made in one go.

Each artifact moves on the next cycle that reaches it, one at a time, upload first and local delete only after the upload is read back and confirmed. At no instant does an artifact have no confirmed readable copy. Nothing here is destructive on its own.
